# Differentiation research brief — "Dharma" → a scripture mastery + generative-catalog product

You are one of three advisors (Gemini 3.1 Pro, Grok 4.2, Claude 4.8) to Anant Swarup, founder
of DharmaWeave. He has a polished but undifferentiated devotional app and wants to make a **bet
on a genuinely differentiated product.** The chosen wedge:

> **A "Duolingo for scripture" — a mastery engine that makes you actually memorize and understand
> the Bhagavad Gita and the Hanuman Chalisa — powered by a generative, ever-growing catalog
> (art, narration, scholarship, music produced on demand).** Two faiths only: Hindu + Buddhist.

## What already exists (assets / moat)
- A beautiful RN/Expo app: temple darshan (swipe deities), sacred calendar, wisdom library,
  audio learning packs (Gita 12 shlokas + full Hanuman Chalisa 42 verses) narrated by a premium
  Hindi voice (ElevenLabs "Kuber"), now **streamed from cloud Storage so the app stays light.**
- A **content factory**: gpt-image-2 (painterly deity art), ElevenLabs (narration), Gemini/Claude
  (scholarship), Suno (devotional music), plus a Postgres DB of 600+ scriptural entries with
  Sanskrit + transliteration + Hindi/English meaning.
- Founder will silent-launch on the App Store and dogfood it himself, adding features, then open it.

## Your task — answer ALL parts, dense markdown, opinionated, numerate. No generic coaching prose.

### PART A — Competitive teardown
Tear down the relevant leaders and say, for each: what they do RIGHT, how they MONETIZE (price,
model, rough scale if known), and where they FAIL / leave whitespace:
- **Hallow** (Catholic prayer app — the faith-app benchmark)
- **Duolingo** (the mastery/gamification mechanics to steal: streaks, SRS, leagues, hearts, XP)
- Meditation incumbents (Calm, Headspace, **Sattva**, **Black Lotus**, Medito)
- Hindu/Sanskrit/Gita apps (Gita apps, Sanskrit-learning apps, Isha/Sadhguru, ISKCON)
- Anything that already attempts "memorize scripture" — does it exist, and is it any good?

### PART B — Differentiation thesis
Why does "mastery engine + generative living catalog" win, and what is genuinely DEFENSIBLE here
(not just nice)? What's the moat? What are the 2-3 biggest risks / ways this fails?

### PART C — v1 wedge spec (the smallest lovable product)
Define the SLP that proves the bet on Gita + Chalisa:
- The core daily loop (what the user does in 3-7 min).
- The 4-6 features to build — and explicitly what NOT to build.
- The gamification mechanics adapted to scripture (spaced repetition, recitation/pronunciation
  scoring, streaks, a culturally-resonant progress metric, leagues/social — say which to skip).
- How the **generative catalog** shows up in v1 concretely (personalization? auto-generated
  practice? on-demand art/audio?).

### PART D — Monetization
Free vs paid split; price points (monthly/annual/lifetime); what actually converts in this
category; whether a learning product monetizes better than a content product and why.

### PART E — Positioning + KPIs
One-sentence positioning ("X is the Y for Z"). The 3-5 KPIs to watch in the silent-launch phase.

Separate the parts clearly.
